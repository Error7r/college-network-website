Establishment

Computer Networks & Web Development

The Department was established in 2011 under the name Networks Management as one of the departments of Computer Science College. Its name was changed to the Department of Computer Networks and Web Development in the academic year 2025–2026.

Graduates of the Department work in the fields of network and internet application programming, website design and development, and learning the fundamentals of network design and connectivity. They also possess expertise in secure data transmission methods across networks using both wired and wireless communication technologies.

Vision

Graduates of the educational program will be leading academic and scientific references at the local and regional levels in the fields of computer networks and web development, contributing to the development of communication technologies, knowledge production, supporting innovation, and exchanging information securely and effectively. They will possess the necessary skills to design, program, and develop websites, internet applications, and networks, as well as to deal with the internet. The program will prepare graduates with the scientific and research competence to keep pace with the rapid developments in information and communication technology.

Mission

  • Providing a stimulating educational and research environment based on recognized academic standards.
  • Providing integrated curricula that cover fundamental principles and keep pace with technological developments.
  • Promoting innovation and scientific research and developing innovative solutions to contemporary problems.
  • Instilling moral values and professional responsibility in students.
  • Promoting academic openness and scientific cooperation to help address global technological challenges.
